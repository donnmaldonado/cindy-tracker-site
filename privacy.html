Privacy Policy

Cindy Tracker · Effective October 14, 2026

The short version

Cindy Tracker collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Your workouts

Your workouts are stored only on your device, in the app’s local storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them, and so does “Reset all workouts” on the Progress screen.

Microphone and speech recognition

Hands-free round logging is optional. If you turn it on, the app asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition.

While it is on, the app listens for you to say “round”. Your speech is turned into text on your device by Apple’s on-device speech recognition (SpeechAnalyzer). Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

The first time you turn it on, iOS may download Apple’s speech model to your device. That download comes from Apple and sends none of your audio.

You can revoke either permission at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
