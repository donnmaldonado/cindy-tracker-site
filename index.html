What it is

Cindy Tracker is an iPhone timer and log for Cindy, a bodyweight workout you can do at home: as many rounds as possible in 20 minutes of 5 pull-ups, 10 push-ups and 15 air squats.

The app times the workout and you log each round as you finish it. You can tap to log, or go hands-free and say “round”. Your results are saved so you can track your progress over time.
